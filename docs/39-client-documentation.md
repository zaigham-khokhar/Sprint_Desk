## What is this system?
A web application that helps teams plan, organize and track their work in one place. Think of it as a shared, always-up-to-date to-do board for entire projects, with clear owners, deadlines and history.

## What can people do with it?
- Create projects and break them into tasks, bugs and stories.
- Assign work to team members and track progress on a visual board.
- Plan work in short cycles called **sprints** and see how much is completed.
- Discuss work through comments, mention colleagues, and attach files.
- Get notified when something needs their attention.
- Track time spent and view reports.
- See deadlines on a calendar.

## Main modules
| Module | In plain words |
|---|---|
| Workspaces & teams | Your company's private area, with people and teams |
| Projects | A collection of related work |
| Issues | Individual pieces of work (task, bug, story) |
| Board | Columns showing work moving from "To Do" to "Done" |
| Backlog & sprints | A prioritized list of upcoming work and time-boxed work cycles |
| Comments & files | Conversations and documents attached to work |
| Notifications | Alerts by app and email |
| Reports & dashboard | Charts and summaries of progress |
| Time tracking | Record hours worked |
| Calendar | Deadlines in one view |

## User roles
| Role | What they can do |
|---|---|
| Owner | Everything in the company workspace, including billing |
| Admin | Manage people and projects |
| Project Manager | Plan sprints and manage a project's workflow and reports |
| Developer / Member | Create and update work, comment, log time |
| Viewer / Client | See progress; read-only |

## Security
Each company's data is completely separated from others. Access is controlled by roles, sign-in can be protected with two-factor authentication, uploaded files are scanned, and every important change is recorded in an audit history. Data is encrypted in transit and backed up daily.

## Reports
Sprint progress (burndown), team speed (velocity), workload per person, issues by status, time logged versus estimated, and exports to spreadsheet.

## Project management
Every project has a lead, a customizable workflow (the steps work goes through), team members with roles, a dashboard and a full activity history.

## Expected business value
| Benefit | Result |
|---|---|
| One source of truth | Fewer lost requests and misunderstandings |
| Live visibility | Less time in status meetings |
| Clear ownership | Faster delivery and accountability |
| Audit history | Easier compliance and dispute resolution |
| Reports | Better planning and forecasting |
